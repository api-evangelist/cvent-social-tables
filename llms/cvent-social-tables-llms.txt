# Social Tables

Cvent Event Diagramming (formerly Social Tables) is a collaborative event design platform that empowers planners and venues to come together and bring their events to life.

## Key Benefits:
- Automate event diagrams and create to-scale layouts within minutes.
- Improve communication with real-time collaboration, commenting, and file storage.
- Take stakeholders on a photo-realistic 3D walkthrough of your event space.
- Manage attendee details with visual seating charts and simple meal management.
- Generate more leads from your website instantly with interactive content.
- Accelerate event planning and increase lead generation.
- Win your customers over by showcasing their event in Photo-Realistic 3D.

## Features:
- Diagram Automation.
- Photorealistic 3D
- Real-time Collaboration.
- Registration Integration
- Post-Event Reports
- Attendee Management
